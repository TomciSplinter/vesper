The problem

A public wallet is a statement anyone can read.

Every stock you hold, what it's worth and everyone you pay sit in plain view for anyone who has your address. Fine for a smart contract. Not for your portfolio.

Vesper keeps what you hold below the horizon, and shows the chain only what it has to.

Safety

Built so you don't have to trust us.

Vesper is a set of open contracts on Robinhood Chain and an app that runs in your browser. This is what keeps your stock safe.

Proofs are made on your device

Your private keys come from your wallet's signature and never leave your browser. No server ever sees your balance.

No admin can touch your stock

The contracts can't be upgraded. Admin powers only limit new deposits: nothing an admin does can move your stock or stop you taking it out.

Changes wait three days

Any change that loosens a rule is announced on-chain and waits three days. The admin is a 2-of-3 multisig.

Screened deposits

Deposits from wallets on the US sanctions list are never approved. Money that isn't approved can only go back where it came from.

Open and verified

All the code is public, and the pool and verifier contracts on the chain are verified against it on Sourcify.

A public key ceremony

The proving key comes from a public ceremony, sealed with a drand randomness beacon, on top of the Perpetual Powers of Tau.

Questions

Good to know.

What can the public see?

Adding stock is a normal public transfer from your wallet into Vesper. After that, a private send or a link shows only that a private transfer of that stock happened: never the amount, the sender or the recipient. A withdrawal shows the receiving address and the amount, but not which private balance it came from.

Do I need a new wallet or seed phrase?

No. Connect the wallet you already use, like MetaMask, Rainbow, Trust or Coinbase Wallet, and sign one free message. That signature becomes your private Vesper account, and the same wallet always opens the same account.

What does it cost?

Opening Vesper is free. Adding stock costs a few cents of ETH in network fees. Private sends and withdrawals through the relayer cost about $1, paid in the stock itself, so your wallet never has to appear. A link costs two of those: one to create it and one to cover the claim.

How do links work?

A link holds the stock privately until someone opens it. Whoever has the link can claim it, so send it the way you'd hand over cash in an envelope. They can claim into their own private balance or straight to a wallet address, without needing any ETH. If nobody claims it, you can take it back from Your links.

Who can see my balance?

Only you, and anyone you give your view key to. A view key shows your balance and history but can't move anything, which is handy for an accountant.

How do you keep out stolen or sanctioned money?

Every deposit has to be approved before it can be withdrawn privately, and deposits from wallets on the US sanctions list are never approved. Money that isn't approved can only go back, publicly, to the wallet that deposited it.

What if this website goes away?

Your stock sits in the pool contract on Robinhood Chain, not on this website, and no admin can lock it. The app is open source and runs entirely in your browser, so anyone can run it, and your account comes back from your wallet's signature.

Is Vesper part of Robinhood?

No. Vesper is an independent project built on Robinhood Chain. It works with the stock tokens available there.
